# QA: deictic-highlighter

About five minutes. Type or paste: "I think you should go there now, sir. However, the doctor left yesterday. Here is my idea."

1. Confirm "I", "you", "my" are one colour (personal), "there" and "Here" another (spatial), "now" and "yesterday" another (temporal), "However" another (discourse), and "sir" and "doctor" another (social). Confirm ordinary words are uncoloured.
2. Type "where", "hereby" and "overhead" in a sentence. Confirm "where" and "overhead" are not coloured, and "hereby" is coloured as discourse, not spatial. Type "HERE," and "(now)" and confirm both are coloured.
3. Type "it's", "I'm" and "ma'am". Confirm "ma'am" is coloured (social) and "it's" and "I'm" are coloured whole (personal).
4. Make part of "today" bold. Confirm the whole word is still coloured. Delete the bold and confirm nothing changes.
5. Edit a coloured word to something else and back. Confirm the colour disappears and returns at the right place, and text typed before it doesn't shift colours onto the wrong words.
6. Check the colours work in headings, list items and quotes. Select and copy a coloured sentence, paste it elsewhere, and confirm the pasted text has no colour of its own and the saved document is unchanged.
7. Open the legend from the toolbar, and confirm each colour matches its category.
8. Open a second pane on the same document and edit in one. Confirm both show matching colours. Turn the module off and on and confirm the colours vanish and return.
