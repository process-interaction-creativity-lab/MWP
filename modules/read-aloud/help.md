# read-aloud: help

You want to hear your draft to catch clumsy rhythm, proofread by ear or rest your eyes. Place the caret and your device's voice reads from there to the end.

## What it gives you

- **Read aloud from cursor** (toolbar button **Read aloud**, palette, **Cmd-Shift-S** or **Ctrl-Shift-S**): reads from the start of your selection (or caret) to the end of the document, in the pane you are working in.
- **Stop reading** (palette, and the toolbar button, which turns into **Stop** while it speaks): ends the reading. **Any key** you press also stops it.
- **Voice** (picker in the toolbar): lists your device's voices and "default voice". Your choice is remembered across reloads.

## Walkthrough

1. Write or open a few paragraphs.
2. Click in the text where you want to start.
3. Press **Cmd-Shift-S** (**Ctrl-Shift-S** elsewhere), or press **Read aloud**. The voice starts at the caret.
4. Listen for places where the rhythm stumbles. Paragraph breaks are spoken as pauses.
5. Press any key to stop. Press **Stop** in the toolbar if you prefer.
6. Open the **Voice** picker, choose a different voice, and start again.

## How the pieces fit

Reading never edits anything: no change to the text, no undo step, no change to your selection. The speech is the text as it was when you started, so you can keep editing while it reads. Starting again while it reads restarts instead of overlapping. The voice belongs to you, not to a document.

## Tips

- It stops by itself at the end of the text, when its pane closes, and when the module is turned off. If the caret is at the very end, nothing starts.
- Notes (footnotes, margin notes, alternatives) are not read as body text.
- A bare Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Cmd press, or a held key repeating, does not stop it.
- Where the device has no speech, the button says **No speech** and is greyed.
