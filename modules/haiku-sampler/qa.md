# QA: haiku-sampler

About five minutes. Have a document with at least three paragraphs of ordinary prose, one heading, and one very short paragraph such as "The cat sat."

1. Put the caret in the first prose paragraph and open the command palette. Confirm a haiku command exists. Run it and confirm a centred card shows three lines. Confirm the first line starts with a capital, no line ends with a comma, full stop or dash, and the words are your own, in your order.
2. Count syllables aloud. Confirm roughly five, seven and five (a miss by one on odd words is fine).
3. Press Again several times. Confirm the haiku changes and the two five-syllable lines are never the same phrase. Press Copy, paste somewhere, and confirm three lines.
4. Close with the Close button; reopen and close with Esc; reopen and close by clicking outside. Confirm the document is unchanged and your caret is where it was each time.
5. Put the caret in "The cat sat." and run it. Confirm a haiku from the text after it, or a message asking for more text, and no error. Try an empty document: a message that nothing was found. Select one sentence and confirm the haiku uses only it.
6. Put the caret in the heading and run it. Confirm it samples from the heading onward.
7. Focus a second pane on a different document and run it. Confirm the haiku comes from that text, and Again keeps using it. With no pane focused, confirm the command is greyed.
8. Turn the module off and on. Confirm the button and command disappear and return, and nothing is left on screen.
