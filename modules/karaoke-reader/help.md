# karaoke-reader: help

You want to hear your draft, not only read it. karaoke-reader speaks the whole document in your device's voice and highlights each word as it is said, so your eye never loses its place.

## What it gives you

- **Read** (toolbar button; also in the palette as "Read the document aloud, highlighting each word"): starts reading the whole document from the top, in the pane you pressed it in.
- **Stop** (the same toolbar button while reading; also in the palette as "Stop reading"): ends the speech and clears the highlight.
- **The highlighted word** (the text itself): one word at a time is marked as it is spoken, moving forward through the text.

## Walkthrough

1. Turn the module on and write or open a document with a few paragraphs.
2. Click **Read** in the toolbar. Your device starts speaking from the first word.
3. Follow the highlight. Between paragraphs there is a short pause, and the last word of one paragraph is never run into the first of the next.
4. While it reads, fix a typo or add a word before the spoken place. The highlight still lands on the word being spoken. If you delete the word that is about to be spoken, nothing is highlighted for it and the reading carries on.
5. Click **Stop** (the button has changed its name) to end the reading early. Otherwise it stops by itself at the end of the text.
6. Click into another pane or document while it reads: the speech keeps going in the pane where you started it.

## How the pieces fit

There is one button: it says **Read** when idle and **Stop** while reading. The highlight is only a pointer to the spoken word, and it disappears when the speech ends. Nothing is changed in your text, and nothing is remembered or saved with the file.

## Tips

- If your device has no speech, the button says **No speech** and is greyed out. It is also greyed out when no pane is focused, and nothing happens on an empty document.
- Notes inside the text, such as footnotes and margin notes, are not read or highlighted.
- Closing the pane or turning the module off also ends the speech.
- Reading adds nothing to undo: Cmd-Z undoes only your last edit. To read from the caret instead, see read-aloud.
