# QA: writor

About five minutes, with AI configured. Paste a short argumentative essay (five or more paragraphs) into a document, then place the module on its pane.

1. Confirm a writor side tab opens at the window's right, offering *Get feedback*, and the text is not covered. Click it: confirm *Set goals* opens first. Fill in the three answers, run *Suggest goals*, confirm five goals arrive (the last about the reader), tick three and add one of your own, then finish. Press Cmd-Z in the text: confirm the goals and answers go in one step, and Cmd-Shift-Z brings them back. Confirm the view's header shows the goals and expands to list them.
2. Run *Get feedback* and, while the spinner shows, retype one sentence of the essay. Confirm up to five violet and three green highlights appear, each on a whole sentence, with cards in document order; that no comment contains a rewritten sentence; and that the view says if a comment was dropped. Press Cmd-Z: confirm it undoes your typing, not the highlights.
3. Click a highlight: confirm its card is marked and scrolled into view. Click another card: confirm the editor scrolls to its sentence with the caret at its start, and that typing goes into the text. Tick *Addressed* on two cards: confirm they and their highlights dim and the header reads "2 / N addressed". Click in the text and press Cmd-Z: confirm the last tick is undone (the card and highlight un-dim, "1 / N").
4. Select a phrase, choose *Ask about this* in the bubble, ask a question. Confirm a new card anchored to that phrase answers it. Open a card's chat, send two messages and use *Find examples*: confirm replies stay on that comment, ask rather than write for you, and the example is quoted from your draft or clearly an analogy. Press Cmd-Z in the text: confirm your messages and the replies stay.
5. Edit inside a highlighted sentence: confirm the highlight follows it. Delete a whole highlighted sentence: confirm its card shows "text removed"; Cmd-Z and confirm the highlight returns on the same card. Then press the card's *Remove* and Cmd-Z: confirm the card comes back.
6. Split the pane (resizable-split) and open the same document in the second pane with the module placed. Click between the two: confirm the view follows the pane you're in, with the same goals, cards, ticks and highlights, a tick in one shows in the other, and each pane keeps its own marked card. Reload: confirm goals, cards, chats and ticks are kept, and the tab is open at the size you left it. Copy the file (export it and open the copy): confirm the copy has the same goals, cards and chats.
7. Turn AI off: confirm *Suggest goals*, *Get feedback*, *Ask about this* and chat grey out with a note, while navigation and *Addressed* still work. Turn it back on.
8. Remove the module from the pane: confirm its tab and the highlights go and the text is unchanged. Place it again: confirm the cards and highlights come back.
