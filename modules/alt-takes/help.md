# alt-takes: help

You are trying several ways of saying the same thing and want to see them side by side while you write. This module lets you mark a stretch of text, keep your favourite in the prose, and park the other versions as cards beside the page.

## What it gives you

- **Mark alternatives** (selection bubble, with a shuffle icon; palette action): marks the selected text, inside one paragraph, as having alternatives. The selected text becomes the first version. A marked span with no alternatives shows a faint dotted underline; with alternatives it shows a soft tint and underline.
- **Cards** (a row beside the page, level with the span, when your caret is inside it): each card is one alternative. The version in the prose is not shown as a card.
- **Use this version** (click a card): swaps that version into the prose; the old live text takes its place among the cards.
- **Add alternative** (the **+** card at the end of the row): opens a small field "Another way". **Enter** adds it, **Shift-Enter** makes a line break, **Esc** cancels.
- **Delete alternative** (the **×** on a card): removes that card. The live text can never be deleted.
- **Remove mark** (button beside the row; palette action **Remove alternatives mark**): ends the marking and keeps whatever text is live.

## Walkthrough

1. Write a few paragraphs and select a phrase in one of them. Click **Mark alternatives** in the bubble that appears.
2. The phrase gets a dotted underline. Click inside it: a row with just a **+** card appears at the right of the page.
3. Click **+**, type another wording and press **Enter**. Add two or three more; the underline turns into a tint.
4. Scroll sideways over the row with the wheel or trackpad if it is long.
5. Click a card: its text replaces the phrase and the old phrase becomes a card. Press Cmd-Z to swap back.
6. Edit the live text by typing in it. The cards stay as they are. If you retype the wording of a card, no duplicate appears.
7. Delete cards you no longer want, then click **Remove mark** when you have decided.

## How the pieces fit

Marking, the card row and the swap are one loop: mark a phrase, collect versions, try each one in place, then remove the mark. Alternatives keep their formatting, and they are saved with the file, travel with copy and paste, and are seen by anyone who opens it. With two panes on one document, the row shows in the pane your caret is in, and a change in one shows in the other.

## Tips

- A selection that crosses paragraphs, or is only spaces, is not offered **Mark alternatives**.
- Add, delete, swap and remove can all be undone.
- With the module off, the live text reads normally and the alternatives stay in the file, back when you turn it on again.
- Nothing is sent anywhere; no AI is involved.
