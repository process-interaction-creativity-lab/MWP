# QA: glas-columns

About five minutes. Have a document with four or five paragraphs of text, and no other layout modules active.

1. Place the module on a pane showing that document. Confirm all paragraphs show in a left column with no right-column content yet, and an "add note" control sits beside each paragraph (the palette has an add-note action too). Confirm you can still type and select as usual and the toolbar still works.
2. Add a note to the second paragraph and type a sentence. Confirm the caret lands in the new note, the note shows in the right column level with the paragraph, and the later paragraphs are not pushed out of alignment. Try the palette action in the fourth paragraph. Add a second note to the same paragraph and confirm both notes show together in the right column, in order, and the paragraph and later rows stay aligned. With a second pane on the document, type in it, then click an "add note" control in the first pane and press Undo: the note goes, the typing stays.
3. In the first note, put the caret in the middle of a sentence and press Enter. Confirm it becomes two notes in the same group. Press Enter in an empty last note twice and confirm the caret never leaves the notes. Press Undo and confirm each step undoes.
4. Put the caret at the end of the first paragraph and press Down. Confirm it goes to the next paragraph, not into the notes. Then go to the end of a group of notes and press Down, and confirm it goes to the next group of notes. Press Up at the start of a paragraph and confirm it goes to the previous paragraph's end. Inside a long wrapped paragraph, confirm the arrows move line by line as normal.
5. Put the caret at the start of the first note and press Backspace; at the end of the last note press Delete. Confirm nothing is pulled out of or into the notes. Put the caret at the start of the paragraph after a group and press Backspace: the group is selected, not deleted; press again and it is gone, and Undo brings it back.
6. Switch a pane on the document to cards-view, drag the card of a paragraph that has notes to another place, and switch back: the notes follow their paragraph. Select a paragraph together with its notes and cut and paste them elsewhere. Confirm the notes are still beside their paragraph. Delete a paragraph that sits between two paragraphs with notes (in a pane without the module, select the whole paragraph block and delete it, so no empty one is left): the two groups are drawn as one in the right column with no gap, "add note" on the paragraph before them adds at the end of the second, and the arrow keys cross between them. Open a second pane on the same document and confirm edits in either pane appear in both.
7. Turn the module off. Confirm each group of notes shows as an opaque placeholder and nothing is lost. Turn it on and confirm the columns return as before. Reload and confirm the same.
8. Narrow the pane. Confirm text and notes both stay reachable and readable, and the page does not break.
