# QA: sentence-length

About five minutes. Write two or more paragraphs with short sentences (two or three words), medium ones, one very long one, and a heading.

1. Every body sentence is tinted; the heading is not. The shortest sentence in the document is red, the longest magenta, the in-between ones run through yellow, green and blue, and equal lengths share a colour. The scale spans the whole document, not each paragraph.
2. The toolbar shows a strip like "Sentences 23 · ■ 7  ■ 18  ■ 41 words": the sentence total (count them) and the shortest, median and longest word counts beside swatches in matching colours. Hovering a swatch names it ("Shortest: 7 words", "Median: …", "Longest: …"). There is nothing to open or close; click into the text and type, and it stays and updates. The toolbar does not grow taller.
3. Type a very long sentence at the end. Tints and legend update as you type, the caret doesn't move, and earlier colours shift. Undo: only your typing is undone.
4. Empty a paragraph, leave another with only spaces, then delete everything: blank paragraphs have no tint, and with no sentences the strip reads "Sentences 0". Add one sentence: it is red.
5. Open a second pane on a different document. The strip shown is the focused pane's own; focusing the first pane brings back its numbers.
6. Reload: the strip is still there. Check light and dark themes.
7. Turn the module off: tints and strip go; on: they return.
