# QA: bubbles-view

About five minutes. Have a document with five or six paragraphs, one heading and one list.

1. Place the module on a pane showing that document. Confirm every block (paragraphs, the heading and the list) shows as its own bubble in a field, nothing is missing, and the bubbles drift slowly and bounce off the edges without leaving the field.
2. Click into a bubble and type. Confirm that bubble stops while it has focus and the text goes into the document. Click the empty field or another bubble and confirm the first drifts again.
3. Use the toolbar and shortcuts inside a bubble (bold, a heading). Confirm they work. Switch the module off and confirm the normal editor shows the same text, with the changes.
4. Back on, put the caret in the middle of a bubble and press Enter, then at its start and press Backspace. Confirm neither splits nor joins bubbles and no text is lost. Press Enter in the middle of that paragraph in a plain pane on the same document and confirm a new bubble appears.
5. Delete a block's text entirely and then the block itself. Confirm that bubble goes. Add a new block and confirm a new bubble appears somewhere reachable.
6. Open a second pane on the same document without the module. Confirm edits in either pane appear in the other, and that many bubbles drifting does not make typing in that pane slow.
7. If the operating system has "reduce motion" on, confirm bubbles stay still. Reload and confirm the same text, with bubbles in fresh places.
