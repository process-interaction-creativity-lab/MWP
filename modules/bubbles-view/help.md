# bubbles-view: help

You want to loosen up and reread a draft as separate thoughts instead of one long page. bubbles-view shows each paragraph as a small bubble drifting around a field, and you can still edit every one.

## What it gives you

- **One bubble per block** (the pane): every paragraph, heading and list item gets its own bubble, and nothing is hidden.
- **Editing in a bubble** (click into it): type as usual. The toolbar, other modules on the pane and their shortcuts work inside a bubble, and Cmd-Z undoes your typing.
- **Drifting** (the field): bubbles move slowly and bounce off the edges; they may pass over each other. The bubble you are working in stands still until you click away.
- **Scrolling in a bubble**: a long paragraph makes its bubble taller up to a limit, then it scrolls inside.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the menu on a pane's tab and turn on **bubbles-view** for it. Your document appears as a field of bubbles.
2. Watch them drift. Each one is a block of your document, in order of its place in the text.
3. Click into a bubble and type. It holds still while you work, and your words go straight into the document.
4. Click the empty field or another bubble: the first one drifts again.
5. Try bold or a heading from the toolbar inside a bubble.
6. Press Enter in a bubble: nothing splits. To add a paragraph, open a second pane on the same document without bubbles-view and press Enter there; a new bubble appears.
7. Delete a block's text and then the block: its bubble goes away.
8. Turn bubbles-view off to get the ordinary page back with the same text.

## How the pieces fit

The bubbles are your real document, not copies: edits in a bubble or in another pane show up in both. Where each bubble floats is just for looking; it is not saved, and after a reload the bubbles start in fresh places.

## Tips

- Enter does not split a bubble and Backspace at its start does not join it to the one before.
- If your system asks for reduced motion, bubbles stay still.
- A plain pane beside the bubbles stays responsive while they drift.
