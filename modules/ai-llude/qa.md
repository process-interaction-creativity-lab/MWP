# QA: ai-llude

About five minutes, with AI configured. Paste or type a few paragraphs of a story (well over 100 characters), and place the module on the pane.

1. In a fresh document with only a sentence or two, press Tab: confirm no palette opens and *Continue with AI* and *Allude with AI* are greyed out in the palette. Type past 100 characters and confirm they enable.
2. Put the caret at the end of a paragraph and press Tab. Confirm the palette opens at the caret with *Continue with AI* and *Allude with AI* (and *Indent* if word-processor is placed); Down, then Up, moves the highlight; Esc closes it with nothing changed. Press Tab again and pick *Continue*: confirm a placeholder shows at the caret, then purple text arrives there that continues the story, with a space before it. Press Cmd-Z: confirm exactly the purple text goes, in one step.
3. Pick *Allude* from the palette. Confirm four short fragments joined by "..." arrive in purple. Click into the middle of the purple text and type a few words: confirm your words are plain and the purple around them stays purple. Delete part of it and confirm nothing else changes.
4. Pick *Continue* and, before the answer arrives, keep typing at the caret. Confirm your typing isn't lost or reordered, the AI text lands at the placeholder (before what you typed), and one Cmd-Z removes just the AI text. Pick again and press Esc while waiting: confirm nothing is inserted.
5. Open a second pane on the same document with the module placed. Pick a suggestion in the first pane: confirm the placeholder shows only there, and the purple text appears in both panes. Reload: confirm the purple text is still purple.
6. Turn AI off (or point it at a bad URL): with AI off, confirm the actions grey out and Tab no longer offers them; with a bad URL, confirm a short "try again" note shows at the caret and nothing is inserted. Turn AI off while a request is out and confirm nothing arrives.
7. Remove the module from the pane: confirm the purple colour goes and the text is unchanged. Place it again and confirm the same spans are purple again.
