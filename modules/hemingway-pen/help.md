# hemingway-pen: help

You over-use adjectives and adverbs and would rather strike them out with a flourish than hunt for them. Switch on the pen, then drag the mouse over your text like a paintbrush.

## What it gives you

- **Pen** (toolbar toggle, or palette "Toggle Hemingway pen"): turns the pen on and off. It is always off when the app starts.
- **Put the Hemingway pen away** (palette, **Esc**, only while the pen is on): turns it off.
- **Painting**: with the pen on, a see-through layer covers the whole window and the pointer is a crosshair. Dragging leaves red and purple splashes that fade within a couple of seconds.
- **Striking**: a stroke over an adjective or adverb deletes it, with the space and any comma, semicolon, colon or closing bracket or quote right after it. A small red flash marks each one.

## Walkthrough

1. Type a sentence full of modifiers: "The very old, rusty gate creaked loudly in the cold wind."
2. Press **Pen** in the toolbar. The pointer turns to a crosshair.
3. Press the mouse and drag across the sentence. Paint trails behind it; "very", "old", "rusty", "loudly" and "cold" disappear.
4. Release the button and move on to another paragraph, or into another pane: one stroke can cross several panes.
5. Press **Esc** to put the pen away. Your text, caret and scrolling work as usual again.
6. Press **Cmd-Z** to bring struck words back one at a time.

## How the pieces fit

The pen has one job: it finds the word under the brush and deletes it if it looks like an adjective or adverb. The paint is only decoration and is never part of your document. Each struck word is its own edit, so undo and redo work word by word. Nothing is remembered between sessions.

## Tips

- While the pen is on, clicks, dragging and the mouse wheel do not reach the page, so a stroke cannot select text or scroll. Press **Esc** to get the page back.
- Which words count is a best guess from one word at a time, so expect some misses and mistakes. Nouns, verbs, numbers and a lone letter are never struck, nor are notes and alternatives from other modules.
- A sentence's final full stop, question mark or exclamation mark is kept.
