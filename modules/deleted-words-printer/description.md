# deleted-words-printer: description

Counts each whole word you delete, and after every few cut words prints a small sticker listing them on a Bluetooth thermal printer at your desk. It makes the cost of cutting physical and keeps the discarded words. You can change how many words make a sticker. Needs a printer.

For a writer who likes to make the cost of cutting physical: a desk toy, and a way of keeping the discarded words. It never changes the document. It supports the Phomemo T02 printer over Bluetooth, in a browser that has Web Bluetooth.

**Kind:** extension. It watches editing in ordinary panes and adds toolbar controls; it doesn't change how a pane shows its document.

- **What counts as a deleted word.** A whole word of at least two characters, made only of letters and digits, bounded on both sides (in the text as it was before the deletion) by a space, punctuation or the edge of the text. Cutting "llo" out of "hello" counts nothing, nor does cutting one character. Deleting several words at once counts each, in document order. Punctuation around a deleted word is not part of it ("world!" counts as "world"). A paragraph break is a boundary: the last word of one paragraph and the first of the next are two words. Text that other modules keep inside the text (notes, alternatives) isn't counted.
- **Whose deletions count.** Only the writer's own edits. Undo, redo and edits from another module, an agent or a collaborator never count; a fix another module makes as part of the writer's own edit counts as the writer's. One document in two panes counts each deletion once. Deleting, undoing and deleting the same word again counts it twice.
- **Batches.** Counted words collect, in deletion order, in a batch; a lifetime total keeps growing. The batch size can be set in the toolbar from 1 to 200 (default 5). Out-of-range or non-numeric input is clamped or ignored, never accepted.
- **Printing.** When the batch reaches the batch size, and the printer is connected and not busy, one sticker prints: a "DELETED WORDS" heading, the words one per line, a dash, and a footer with the batch size and the lifetime total; long lines wrap to the sticker. A batch over 40 words (a deleted paragraph) prints a random sample of 40 distinct words. One sticker per batch, however many batches are owed. After a print only the words that were in the batch when it started leave it; words deleted meanwhile wait for the next sticker. The lifetime total never goes down.
- **No printer, no loss.** While the printer is disconnected or busy, words stay in the batch and nothing errors. A full batch prints at once when the printer connects, and otherwise on the first deletion that finds the printer free.
- **Connecting.** A toolbar button opens the browser's Bluetooth chooser and, once connected, disconnects. It shows "connecting" while waiting and is disabled while connecting or printing. A cancelled chooser, missing Bluetooth support, a failed connection and a failed print each show a readable reason in the app, leave the module usable, and keep the batch (the next deletion retries a failed print).
- **The toolbar controls** (connect, batch size) are visible whenever the module is on, and show the connection and the batch size.
- **One printer for the whole app,** shared by every pane and document. Turning the module off, or closing the app, disconnects it; after a reload the writer reconnects by hand.
- **Data.** The batch, the lifetime total and the batch size belong to the writer, across all documents, and are remembered across reloads; none of it travels with a file.
