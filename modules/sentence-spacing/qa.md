# QA: sentence-spacing

About five minutes. Use three paragraphs of several sentences each, one inside a list, and a heading.

1. Click into the first paragraph: its sentences spread apart, the other paragraphs stay tight, and the last sentence has no extra gap. Move the caret to the second paragraph: the spacing follows.
2. Select from the middle of the first paragraph into the second: both are spaced, the third is not.
3. Switch to words from the toolbar: every word in the active paragraph spreads, and the toolbar and palette show words as chosen. Switch back: sentences return.
4. Click into the heading (not spaced), a list paragraph (spaced), and a one-sentence paragraph (no gap in sentence mode).
5. Add an inline item (a footnote or another module's inline part) to a paragraph and click into it in sentence mode: gaps still fall between the right sentences, not words.
6. Type in a spaced paragraph and undo: typing isn't slowed and undo only undoes typing. Copy and paste a spaced paragraph: plain text, no extra spaces.
7. Reload: the chosen mode is still on. Open a second pane on the same document with the other mode: each keeps its own mode and caret spacing.
8. Turn the module off and on: spacing stops and returns.
