# QA: graveyard

About five minutes. Start with a document of a few paragraphs. The graveyard never shows in the writing; check its entries in the saved file (or the dev console: the document's last node).

1. Select one whole word and delete it. Confirm nothing appears in the writing, and the graveyard holds the word as its only entry.
2. Type "big cat" at the end of a paragraph, then Backspace one letter at a time. Confirm "cat" is buried once the whole word is gone, then "big". Delete only part of a word and confirm nothing is buried.
3. Delete a word, then press Cmd-Z once. Confirm the word is back and its entry is gone. Redo and confirm both happen again. Type some text and undo it; confirm nothing is buried.
4. Select all in the writing and delete. Confirm the writing is empty, the graveyard has gained it as one entry, and its earlier entries are still there. Press Delete at the very end of the writing and Backspace at its start; confirm nothing changes.
5. Open a second pane on the same document. Delete a word in one pane and confirm it is buried once.
6. Save, close and reopen the document; confirm the entries are still there. Turn the module off, delete a word, and confirm the graveyard is kept and gains nothing.
