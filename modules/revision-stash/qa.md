# QA: revision-stash

About five minutes. Start with a document of three short paragraphs of several sentences each.

1. Select a single sentence: no stash control. Select from the middle of one sentence to the middle of the next: a stash control appears; open it and confirm the choices are whole sentences.
2. Pick one as the live version. The selected sentences are replaced by only that one, faintly tinted, with a "+N" badge showing the right count. Repeat with a selection across a paragraph break.
3. Click the badge. A panel opens that doesn't stop you clicking in the text. Make an alternative live: they swap places, count unchanged. Undo swaps back; redo it.
4. In the panel, edit, add and delete alternatives; the badge count follows. Delete them all: the text stays tinted and is still in the survey.
5. Edit the live text in place. The earlier wording appears once as an alternative; a second edit adds no copy. Bold or italic inside an alternative survives a switch.
6. Copy the stashed text and paste it elsewhere, then undo and redo: the alternatives come each time. Delete the live text: its alternatives go with it, and undo brings both back.
7. Open "Needs work (n)": it lists every stash, doesn't overlap other corner controls, and choosing one scrolls to it and opens its panel. Resolve one: tint, badge and list entry go, the text stays.
8. Open a second pane on the same document: badges and tint show in both, and a switch in one shows in the other. Turn the module off and on: the alternatives are still there.
