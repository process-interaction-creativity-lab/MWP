# revision-stash: help

You can't decide between two or three phrasings of a passage, and you don't want to lose the ones you don't pick yet. revision-stash lets you keep one version live in the text and fold the others away behind a small count.

## What it gives you

- **Stash alternatives** (selection bubble, palette, or Cmd-Alt-S): offered when your selection touches two or more sentences. The selection snaps outward to whole sentences, even across paragraphs.
- **Which sentence stays?** (dialog): lists the sentences you selected. The one you click becomes the live text, and the others become alternatives.
- **Tint and +N badge** (the text itself): the live text is faintly tinted, and a small badge at its end shows how many alternatives are held. Both show in every pane on the document.
- **Alternatives** (panel beside the text, opened by clicking the badge): shows the **Live** text and each alternative in an editable box, with **Make live** and **Delete** under each. A **New alternative** box with **Add** adds your own, and **Resolve** drops all alternatives, keeping the live text. The **x** button closes the panel.
- **Needs work (n)** (toolbar): lists every stash in the document with its live text and count. Choosing one scrolls to it, selects it and opens its panel.

## Walkthrough

1. Turn the module on and write a paragraph where you phrase the same idea in three sentences one after another.
2. Select from the middle of the first sentence to the middle of the third. A **Stash alternatives** button appears; a selection inside one sentence offers nothing.
3. Click it and, in the dialog, pick the sentence you like best. The three sentences become one, tinted, with a "+2" badge.
4. Click the badge. The panel opens, and you can still click and type in the text.
5. Click **Make live** under an alternative: it and the live text swap places. Cmd-Z swaps them back.
6. Edit an alternative in its box, type in **New alternative** and press Enter or **Add**, or **Delete** one. The badge count follows.
7. Type in the live text itself. Its earlier wording is saved once as an alternative; further edits don't add more copies.
8. When you have decided, click **Resolve**. The tint and badge go and the live text stays. Use **Needs work (n)** any time to find stashes you haven't resolved.

## How the pieces fit

Stashing creates the live text, the badge opens the panel to manage it, and the survey finds every stash you have left. The alternatives are saved with the file, travel with the text on copy and paste, and survive turning the module off and on. Deleting the live text deletes its alternatives too, and Cmd-Z brings both back.

## Tips

- If you delete the last alternative, the text stays tinted and listed in **Needs work (n)**; only **Resolve** clears it.
- Stashing, switching, resolving and panel edits all undo normally.
- Nothing is sent anywhere; no AI is involved.
