# margin-notes: help

You are reviewing your own text, or someone else's, and want to comment without touching a word. margin-notes lets you attach pencilled notes that sit in the page margin beside the line they refer to.

## What it gives you

- **Add note** (selection bubble over selected text, or palette **Add margin note**, Cmd-Alt-M or Ctrl-Alt-M): highlights the selection and opens an empty note in the right margin with the caret in it. It does not appear for a caret alone, or for a selection of only spaces.
- **Note card** (the margin): a small editable card level with the line where its text ends. Type in it directly. Cards follow the text as you scroll and edit, and never overlap.
- **Delete note** (button on the card, shown when you hover): removes the note and its highlight.
- **Highlight** (the text itself): marks the span the note is about. If you split it with Enter or delete from its middle, every remaining piece stays highlighted and the card follows the first piece.
- **Notes** (toolbar toggle, **Show margin notes** in the palette): shows or hides the margin layer for this pane.

## Walkthrough

1. Turn margin-notes on and write a few paragraphs.
2. Select a phrase and click **Add note** in the bubble. The phrase is highlighted and an empty card appears in the margin.
3. Type your comment, then click back into the text. The note is saved when you leave it.
4. Add more notes, including two on nearby lines. The lower card is nudged down so they stay readable.
5. Click into a note: its card and every piece of its highlight take a stronger tint until you leave.
6. Click **Notes** in the toolbar to hide the margin in this pane, and again to bring it back.
7. Hover a card and use **Delete note** to remove one.

## How the pieces fit

A note is a comment on a span of your text. The highlight shows what it is about, the card holds what you say, and the two move together. Notes are saved with the file and appear in every pane showing it, but your words are never changed.

## Tips

- Deleting the text a note is attached to takes the note with it; undo brings text, highlight and note back together.
- Copying highlighted text and pasting it gives the copy its own note with the same body, which you can edit separately.
- Each pane remembers its own show or hide choice across reloads. In a narrow pane, scroll sideways to reach the cards.
- Turn the module off and the notes stay in the file but disappear from view until you turn it on again.
