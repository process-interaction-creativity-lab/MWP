# Welcome to the Modular Word Processor

The Modular Word Processor is a word processor built from modules: small, separate pieces that each add one thing, like a formatting toolbar, a different way to view your document, or a writing tool. You choose which modules are on for each pane, and they work together on the same document, so you can put together the writing tool that suits the task in front of you and change it whenever you like. The modules are user-generated: each one was made by someone using the platform, not built into the word processor, and you can make your own with **Create a module** in the toolbar.

Right now the page is split in two, side by side, by the **resizable-split** module; drag the divider between the halves to resize them. Both halves show this same document, the Tutorial. The left pane is the ordinary text editor with **word-processor** on, which gives you the formatting toolbar and the usual shortcuts. The right pane has **cards-view** in cards mode, showing each paragraph as a card you can drag around, with **word-processor** on there too, so you can format text inside a card. A few more modules are installed but not switched on yet: **topic-sentence-highlighting**, **sentence-cycler**, **hemingway-pen** and **poet-critique**. You turn a module on or off from a pane's tab at the top of the page (**Text Editor** for the left pane, **cards-view** for the right), and the document picker at the left of the toolbar opens another document, such as the opening of *Frankenstein*.

Things to try next:

- Drag a card on the right to a new place, and watch the paragraphs on the left reorder to match.
- Open the **cards-view** tab and turn on topic-sentence-highlighting, to read the cards as a skeleton of their first sentences.
- Open the **Text Editor** tab and turn on sentence-cycler, then hover over a paragraph and click its button to try its sentences in another order.
- Have fun!
